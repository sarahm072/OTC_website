A crew, not just a club

OTC brings together students who want to learn by making things. We run hands-on workshops, build team projects and prepare for robotics and programming competitions.

You do not need to be an expert. You need curiosity, and the will to ship something with other people.

Three ways to build

Pick one, or mix them. Most members do.

Robotics

Design, wire and program machines that move, sense and react.

  • Arduino
  • Sensors
  • Motors

Computer Science

Learn to write clean code, solve problems and build useful software.

  • Web
  • Algorithms
  • AI

Innovation

Turn an idea into a prototype and present it to real people.

  • Prototyping
  • Teamwork
  • Pitching

How to join

  1. Apply online

    Fill the short form when recruitment opens. It takes about five minutes.

  2. Meet the crew

    We contact you to get to know you and answer your questions.

  3. Pick a team

    Join a team that fits your interests and start building with us.

Why join OTC

What you get from being part of the crew.

  • Learn by doing

    Workshops where you build something.

  • Real team projects

    Projects you can show in a portfolio.

  • Mentoring

    Advice from senior members.

  • Competitions

    Robotics and programming contests.

  • Equipment access

    Tools and components hard to get alone.

  • A network

    Meet students and professionals in Algeria's tech scene.
