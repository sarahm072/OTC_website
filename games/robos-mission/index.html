How to play

Controls

  • ←→ or AD  Move
  • Space or ↑  Jump (press twice for a double jump!)
  • X or J  Zap an energy pulse
  • P Pause   M Sound
  • On a phone, use the round buttons on screen.

Your mission

  • ⚡ Collect energy cells to open the exit portal
  • 🐛 Jump on bugs or zap them
  • 💗 Hearts heal you
  • 🖥️ Terminals ask AI and coding questions
  • ⚠️ Spikes, saws and pits hurt
